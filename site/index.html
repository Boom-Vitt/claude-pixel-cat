A step change in depth — reasoning that holds its line across the longest, hardest problems.

Opus 4.8 was built for the work that doesn't fit in a single prompt: multi-step agents, sprawling codebases, and decisions where getting it almost right isn't enough. It plans, it stays on task, and it knows when to slow down.

Capabilities

Range, with intent.

01

Reasoning that holds

Sustained, multi-step thinking that keeps the thread across long, ambiguous problems — and surfaces its assumptions instead of hiding them.

02

Agentic coding

Reads a whole repository, plans a change, runs the tools, and verifies the result — staying coherent across hundreds of steps without losing the plot.

03

Sees & reads

Native multimodal understanding — diagrams, screenshots, dense documents and charts read in context, not as an afterthought.

04

Aligned by design

Trained to be helpful, honest, and careful — clearer refusals, fewer false ones, and judgment that scales with the stakes of the task.
